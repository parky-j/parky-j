## 👨‍🔬 About Me

I am an **AI Ph.D. student** focusing on **Computer Vision** and **Efficient Deep Learning**.

My research primarily explores how to build **smaller, faster, and more reliable neural networks**, with particular interests in **Knowledge Distillation**, **Model Re-parameterization**, **Domain Generalization**, and **Biometric / Physiological Signal Analysis**.

> 🎯 **Research Goal**
> Developing efficient and robust AI models that retain strong representation and generalization capabilities under real-world constraints.
